Entry: [09/28/26]

This being my first entry I'd like to start by saying that this will be where I share my thoughts and opinions on certain topics and concepts. I'm a firm believer in ideas that serve to be functional and practical. This includes everything from the software I use, down to the clothing I wear. I feel like the world has steered so far away from making simple things of quality that last. I pride myself on taking the time to use things that just work with minimal abstraction and repurposing things that most people would find little to no value in. Not only has this belief made me more thoughtful about what I create and consume, but it has also forced me to aquire a lot of skills I never thought I'd learn. Sometimes less really is more.
